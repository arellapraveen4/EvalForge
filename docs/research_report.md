# EvalForge Research Report

## Title

Reliability, Bias and Agreement of LLM-as-a-Judge Systems in Human Preference Evaluation

## Research questions

1. How closely do LLM judges agree with human annotators?
2. Does response ordering influence judge preferences?
3. Which dimensions show the greatest human disagreement?
4. How reliable are judges across coding, reasoning, writing, and general knowledge tasks?
5. How does changing a rubric affect judgments?
6. Can evidence-based evaluation improve groundedness judgments?

## Methodology

Curate a license-reviewed dataset, generate multiple model responses, collect independent blind human annotations, run multiple judge configurations, calculate human and judge reliability, analyze position bias and rubric sensitivity, and publish the raw experiment configuration and limitations.

Do not describe small-sample agreement as proof that an evaluator is superior. Do not treat majority voting as universal ground truth.
